Research

I'm a master's student in computer science at Duke University, advised by Prof. Matthew Lentz. I work on machine learning systems.

I look for places where AI workloads and the hardware beneath them don't fit: work that gets thrown away and redone, state that could have been reused, compute that sits idle. My recent work lets decode run speculatively on invalidated KV cache, so it no longer waits on a fresh prefill. I believe lasting progress in AI will come from software and hardware evolving together, and that is the direction I want to push.

Before Duke, I was an HPC engineer and then a trader at Uranus Research, a quantitative trading firm.

I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.
